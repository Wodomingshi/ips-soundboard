# Investment Policy Statement: Sofia M. (fictional sample investor)

*This investor is invented for testing and demonstration. Any resemblance to a real person is coincidental.*

## Profile
Sofia is 45, lives in Germany and spends in euros. She plans to retire at 65. Her income is stable and she has no large planned withdrawals in the next ten years.

## Objectives
- Earn 3% a year above euro-area inflation over rolling 10-year periods.
- Accept a peak-to-trough loss of up to 30% in a severe crisis without selling.

## Liquidity
Keep at least 6 months of expenses in cash outside the portfolio. Monthly expenses are at most EUR 3,500.

## Scope
The SAA applies to her brokerage account. Her company pension is reported but not managed.

## Strategic asset allocation
| Sleeve | Target | Range |
|---|---|---|
| European equities | 25% | 20–30% |
| World equities ex-Europe | 35% | 30–40% |
| Emerging-market equities | 5% | 2–8% |
| Euro government bonds | 25% | 20–30% |
| Gold | 5% | 2–8% |
| Cash | 5% | 0–10% |

## Limits
- No single stock above 5% of the portfolio when buying; anything above 8% is trimmed.
- Single stocks in total no more than 20%.
- No leverage, short selling, derivatives or leveraged/inverse ETFs.
- No direct holdings in tobacco companies.

## Tactical allocation
Sleeve tilts of up to ±3 percentage points, with a total one-way deviation of at most 6 points. No thematic positions. Tilts are only opened when the price is above its 10-month average.

## Rebalancing
Review quarterly, rebalance annually, and don't trade amounts below EUR 1,000.
